### Soft warnings worth tracking

1. **jsx-a11y absent** — `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y@6.10` does not yet support `eslint@10`. Until a compatible release lands, lint-time accessibility coverage is reduced; Gate 7's runtime axe-core scan is the only a11y signal the gate produces. Options: pin eslint to `^9.x` (broad ecosystem support) or wait for jsx-a11y 6.11+.
2. **Main chunk 687 kB** — Single JS chunk over Vite's 500 kB threshold. Likely react-leaflet + leaflet, since they're the heaviest deps. Consider route-level dynamic `import()` for `/map` and `/normandy` (which use the map). Not a regression from this window — pre-existing on main.
3. **axe-core `incomplete` on `/`** — Manual review candidates:
   - `color-contrast`: 4 nodes. Often fires when text sits on a gradient, image, or partially-transparent background where axe can't compute the effective contrast ratio. Spot-check the 4 nodes in browser DevTools / use a contrast picker on the rendered pixels.
   - `link-in-text-block`: 1 node. Fires when a link inside body text has no underline and relies on color alone to be distinguishable. Verify in [src/index.css](src/index.css) or wherever `.ink-link` is defined that the underline style applies.
